**1.40.0: (released 03/10/2026)**
Modifying certain interface elements has severely compromised the software's security.
Improve AI response time.
Improve software stability.
Integrated the history feature from version 1.40.0 (Software that automatically displays version information from the link: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/letranthienphat/LTTP-Ai-Online/refs/heads/main/information%20about%20update.md)
Helps the software function smoothly between the phone and the computer.
Integrated quick lookup feature. (To enable it, click the icon next to the "delete conversation" button; when enabled, the AI ​​will not retain previous conversation input, and the conversation will be named "Quick Lookup".)
AI usage quotas are automatically allocated based on actual usage and the number of accounts—with guest accounts excluded from the calculation. Guest accounts are limited to a maximum of 150 AI messages per day if there are fewer than five high-frequency user accounts (as determined by AI analysis), or 100 messages per day otherwise—all to ensure that AI non-response errors remain extremely rare.
